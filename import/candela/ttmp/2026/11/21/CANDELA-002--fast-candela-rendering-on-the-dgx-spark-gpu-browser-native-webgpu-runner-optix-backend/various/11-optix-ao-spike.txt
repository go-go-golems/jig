## result
OptiX / WebGPU on the AO workload: 1721 / 105 ≈ 16×.
For comparison, the full path tracer on WebGPU: 13.2 spp/s (K-09 1080p 64 spp, same session).
Caveats: AO is traversal-bound and nearly shading-free; the path tracer also spends time in material evaluation,
light sampling and its per-sample submit/wait, so its OptiX speed-up will be smaller than 16× by an amount this spike
does not measure. The WebGPU figure includes Candela's per-sample dispatch + wait (step 2's batching would raise it).
