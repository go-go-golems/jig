# Housing and optical stack

The end of the rear threaded flange defines zero. Positive Z points toward the subject. The 1956 manufacturer's specification on PDF page 9, upper section, assigns the bracketed dimensions to CN-1210-000. It shows a 57.1 mm housing, 20 mm axial spacing, a 6.1 mm rear projection, M29.5 × 0.5 lens cell threads and an M32.5 × 0.5 mounting thread. The model represents the lens cell bore at the nominal minor diameter. Only the mounting thread has a helical feature. The cross-section shows separate front and rear cell seats, with the rear seat attached to the housing. The archive had run a continuous tube through both moving-leaf planes. The reconstructed rear flange now joins the housing web at Z 6.1–6.7 mm. The front seat starts at Z 11.52 mm on the other side of the leaves. Its partial skirt leaves room for the escapement. Internal lengths and reliefs are inferred.

The reconstruction gives the drawn shell a 0.6 mm wall and places the flat diaphragm envelope at Z 9.2–9.8 mm. Its 24 mm opening agrees with the bracketed diameter on the specification sheet. Wall thickness, internal stack heights and the annular envelope are modeling choices.
