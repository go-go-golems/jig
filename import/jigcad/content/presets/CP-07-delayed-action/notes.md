# V delayed action

Moving the MXV selector to V pulls its post through the setting-lever slot. The lever tensions the drive spring and the one-way clutch spring rides over the sloping notches of the first gear. The locking lever holds the pallet while the shutter is cocked. On release it withdraws, the pallet can vibrate, and the drive spring turns the train. A notch catches the clutch spring and carries the setting lever back. Outside the cartridge the V-latching lever holds the blade ring until the setting lever withdraws it.

**Reconstruction.** Look through the upper plate cutaway to inspect the input end and the pallet end. The screw at the input end passes through a distance pillar between the two plates. Each part is available through `delay.parts.js`. The local origin lies at the input arbor. Axle distances, spring section, outlines, tooth counts and thicknesses are illustrative. The end wheels are separated by space for the intermediate train. The photograph and service description establish their roles but leave the intermediate gearing and exact spring form unresolved. The preset shows the spring in plan and has independent pivots. It does not predict elapsed seconds or establish a mounting transform into the size 0 body.
