# Front controls

The front plate retains the speed controls around the optical axis. In the assembled shutter, turning the exterior setting ring indexes the speed cam beneath the cover. A follower reads that cam to change the travel available to the retard mechanism.

> **Try it.** Use the [side view](lab:view/stage?view=side) to distinguish the axial levels, then inspect the central opening in [plan](lab:view/stage?view=plan).

## What the stack represents

The supplied CAD seats the cam at Z 16.2 mm, the setting ring at 17.2 mm and the plate at 18.6 mm. The outer ring measures 58.5 mm across in the model. These Z levels use the archive's rear datum. The cover and retaining ring share one builder. The speed cam represents its envelope and pivot clearances. The steps, edge knurl and engraved speeds require additional geometry.

Lesson 9 figures 23–26 show removal of the plate and rings. Figures 31–37 show how followers and the cam work together on the 00-MXV course example. Compare those functional illustrations with the archive's separate speedCam, speedRing and cover scripts. The supplied layer dimensions do not establish production tolerances on the 0-MXV.
