# Presser-foot lift

This study opens with the foot raised **6.1 mm above the needle plate datum**.
Change `lift` in Code to 0 and rebuild to inspect the sewing position. Lift is
independent of shaft phase. In SG-03, the needle cycles
while the lowered foot holds its position.

The bar travels vertically in the guide. The bracket clamps onto the bar,
carrying the lower spring seat and the lifting contact. A rear hand lever
raises the bracket. The upper regulating screw holds the opposing seat. Each
rebuild shortens the helical spring by the rise of the foot. The zigzag foot
snaps into the shank by its cross-pin. Its lateral slot admits the needle on
both sides of a stitch. The front guide slot separates the raised toes.

Singer service manual 104 73 14-26, procedure 3-4, printed page 9 (PDF page
10), specifies a 6.0–6.2 mm plate-to-sole gauge. The full-size datapoint used
here is the 6.1 mm midpoint of that interval. The family parts drawing on
plate E and its list on printed pages 10–11 identify the following items.

The guide represents the head's local bearing locations. The 22 mm distance
from needle axis to bar axis, the bar diameter, both lever arms, the spring
wire diameter, turns, seat heights and the foot outline are reconstruction
parameters. The check uses a rigid foot on an empty plate. The model omits
thread profiles, the screw washer, tension-release linkage, spring stresses,
fabric compliance and snap-jaw flexure. Screws show nominal-length envelopes
and simplified mating features. The 0–6.2 mm input range bounds this study
to the documented service setting.
