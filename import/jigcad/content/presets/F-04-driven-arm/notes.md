# Driven-arm reactions

A motor sweeps an aluminium arm through a full turn at 120 rpm. Gravity and a 2 N downward force at the tip act throughout the cycle. The assembly study calculates the force carried by the hinge and the torque required at the drive.

> **Try it.** Open **Mechanism** and compare `F:hinge` in N with `T:hinge` in N·mm. Click along a plot to move the arm to that angle. In Code, double the speed to 240 rpm and compare the reaction as the centripetal acceleration increases.

This preset shows the forces a mechanism transmits. [Slider-crank rod](lab:preset/F-05) takes those kinds of loads into a stress solve on an individual component.
