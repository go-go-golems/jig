# Flash contact study

One contact lever completes the circuit on both settings. The grounding strip maintains contact with the moving lever. At the instant of firing, the lever reaches the terminal strip connected to the flash socket. This isolated, open-contact pose shows the two levers on a common post axis.

> **Try it.** Inspect the space between the free end and the terminal in [plan](lab:view/stage?view=plan). From the [side](lab:view/stage?view=side), locate the lower M-closing lever and the upper lever's downward lug.

## Reading the mechanism

On M, the spring-loaded lower lever moves the upper contact lever as the blade ring starts moving. On X the blade operating ring actuates the contact lever at full opening. Lesson 9 figures 81–82 identify the two strips, figure 109 shows the two loose levers and figures 110–112 show their order and interacting lugs.

## Transfer to the size 0

The course photographs depict a 00-MXV. The silhouettes and stack order here follow those photographs. Dimensions, colours and the open gap are illustrative. The origin is local to the contact pivot. Installing this group into the size-0 shutter requires identifying the exact variant, locating the post and socket relative to the optical axis, and measuring the lug heights and contact angles. The M delay sector, selector latch and lever springs require separate measurements. This preset models the contact stack in one pose and supplies no timing or electrical simulation.
