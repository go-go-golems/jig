The home pose shows the needle rising on the left with its point 5 mm above
the plate. Section 3-5 of the 44S service manual specifies a 3–5 mm check at
width dial 6. Section 3-7 calls for equal edge clearance on the two drops.
The model uses a nominal ±3 mm at the plate and the 5 mm setup value.

Press Play and enable Loop to inspect successive drops. The return window
uses the same height on the descending stroke. That symmetry and the cosine
transfer are modeling choices. The actual cam profile is unmeasured.
