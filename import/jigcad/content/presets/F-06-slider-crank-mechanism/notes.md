# Slider-crank mechanism

This is the four-part source assembly for the [slider-crank rod stress study](lab:preset/F-05). The frame holds the crank's pivot and the slider's guide. The crank turns, the rod links the crank pin to the wrist, and the slider travels along the guide.

> **Try it.** [Open Mechanism](lab:show/mechanism) and click along the **cycle** plots to pose the assembly. Compare `F:pin` and `F:wrist`, the loads at the two ends of the rod, and `T:main`, the torque at the crank. [Pose the crank at 90°](lab:joint/main=90) to match the explicit-angle exercise in the rod study.

Select **View rod stress study** in the Stage to return to the isolated rod and its stress overlay. The assembly's displayed pose and the stress study's load angle are controlled independently.
