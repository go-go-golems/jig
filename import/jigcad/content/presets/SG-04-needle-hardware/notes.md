drive bracket on the bar. The screw projects to the operator's right. Its
driver slot opens on the outer head. The flat back of the needle enters a
blind axial socket and the eye opens from front to rear.

The selectable components correspond to Singer family plate E positions 18,
19, 20, 35 and 50. The needle represents a Schmetz 130/705 H universal NM90.
The manufacturer specifies a 2.04 mm shank and 33.9 mm from butt to top of
eye. The blade diameter is 0.90 mm. The photos do not determine the installed
needle brand or gauge. The eye aperture, groove, scarf, screw, socket,
clamp and bracket dimensions are reconstruction assumptions. Screw thread
forms are omitted. The fit and motion of this model are study results.
