Try it. Load D-03, switch to front elevation, slow time to a quarter and play a kickflip. Watch the trail grow in the air and stay after the landing; count the seven ghosts. Then play a pop shove-it and look from above to see the nose swing.

Exercise. Set cruise/lean to −0.5 and cruise/speed to 4 m/s. Write down which way the board will turn and the radius you expect, then measure the radius as speed divided by yaw rate.

Careful. peakSpin mixes flight and landing. To compare tricks by how fast they spin in the air, stop at touchdown (the first wheel impact) or read recognizer/spinRate while flick/flipping is true.
