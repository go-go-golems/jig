# Screens

Screenshots of the real stage in B-01 (Flatground) with the autopilot riding, cropped to the stage, 940 × 664 px. Use them to check a new view or a re-implementation of the look against the original; they are references, not artwork for a page.

- `stage-axo.png`: the default axonometric view after a kickflip: trail ghosts and paths, the floor dimension, hatched shadow, chips, the trick toast, the scale bar and the title block. Light theme.
- `stage-side-track-kickflip.png`: side elevation with Track on, just after a kickflip lands: the ground line and its hatched band, the trail seen end-on, B-01's funbox in hatch. Light theme.
- `stage-plan.png`: the plan, looking straight down: the rider from above, the shadow, the grid, the trail and its dimension at the edge. Light theme.
- `stage-overlays.png`: axonometric at zoom 1.9 with Forces, Contacts, Rig, Sites and Course on: every overlay hue at once. Light theme.
- `stage-trail-dark.png`: front elevation in the dark theme just after a kickflip: the seven ghost decks, the blue centre path and the dashed teal nose path, light ink on the dark sheet.
