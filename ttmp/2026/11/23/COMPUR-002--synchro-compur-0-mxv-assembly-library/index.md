Use the [intern guide](design-doc/01-building-the-synchro-compur-0-mxv-in-jigcad.md) to locate the service evidence and follow a part from a plate into the workbench. The [reconstruction playbook](playbook/01-reconstruct-a-shutter-from-service-plates.md) gives the repeatable procedure. The working baseline is the CN-1210-000 envelope. Companion presets isolate the operating groups. Some groups borrow topology from 00-MXV course photographs and have only a local datum. See the notes inside each preset for the scope of the transfer.
