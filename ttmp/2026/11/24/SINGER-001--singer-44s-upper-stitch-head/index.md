# Singer 44S upper stitch head


Three Jig presets share one parts library. Open SG-01 for the centered needle drive, SG-02 for needle swing, and SG-03 for the coupled upper head. In Code, change the shaft phase or gate amplitude and rebuild. The preset units are degrees. At zero amplitude the needle stays centered.

The [source and CAD comparison](reference/01-source-frames-and-upper-head-geometry.md) contains six archive images, four workbench views, and both solved trajectories. The [diary](diary/01-singer-upper-head-implementation-diary.md) records the geometry assumptions, a corrected lever branch, and the validation results. Physical verification is tracked in [tasks](tasks.md).
